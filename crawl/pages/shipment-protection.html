How Stord Shipment Protection Works

1

Seamless E-Comm Checkout Flow

With customizable options, you can integrate Stord Shipment Protection into your existing checkout flow quickly and easily. Configure your protection in the cart, earlier in the shopping journey, to target higher attach rates. Configure your protection to be covered for all customers by you, or as an optional opt-in selection at Cart or Checkout:

  • Cost of protection is automatically calculated based on the total value of the cart
  • Orders with Stord Shipment Protection are automatically registered in Stord's OMS
  • Choose where to insert the Shipment Protection offering in your checkout flow to match the experience you prefer
2

Fast Claims Process

Customers can file and track claims on your branded order tracking portal. This process takes seconds for the customer and ensures your brand message is maintained from start to finish.

  • Self-service claims portal means automatic claim filing, bypassing your customer service team
  • Track filed claims directly in Stord's OMS
  • Review and easily create suitable responses to claims
3

Replacement Orders

As soon as a claim is submitted you can begin reviewing to determine the best course of action. You will receive the payout directly, granting you the ability to more quickly re-ship the products or reimburse your customer:

  • Re-ship products before reimbursement for a better customer experience
  • You are in control to issue fast refunds or ship replacement products with ease

Instant Notifications

Keep your customers updated on the status of the claim with automated and personalized emails. No confusion or added work on your customer support team.

  • Review the number of claims, approvals, and denials all at a glance
  • Track performance with key metrics like impressions, add-to-cart rate, and more
