Data Driven Inserts

Your customer data already tells you what each shopper wants. Unbox turns it into messaging that matches. Pulling from your sales channels, CRM, and order history, Unbox builds offers and recommendations based on how someone really shops, not a generic template everyone else gets.

    Drag and Drop Design

    No designer? No problem. With a simple drag and drop editor, you can easily upload brand approved elements to your resource library to create unique and compelling messaging for each customer instantaneously. Every insert is perfectly on brand while also being 100% unique to each customer.

      Track, Print, Improve

      Launch a new offer, see how it performs, and swap it out, all in the same day, with no reprint minimums and no stuck inventory. Because every insert prints on demand, your message is never out of date, even when your campaign changes overnight.

      Now your physical inserts have the data, flexibility, speed, and customization previously reserved for your digital ads.

        Turn Gift Notes Into New Customers

        Every Gift Is a New Audience

        Someone buys from you and ships it to a person who’s never heard of your brand. That recipient opens the box, reads a personal message, and meets you for the first time. Gift notes turn that moment into an acquisition channel instead of a missed one. Available for Shopify Plus users.

        Personal Message, Your Brand

        The message your customer writes prints alongside your artwork, automatically, at the pack station. Add a QR code and the same card that says “Happy Birthday” also carries a first-purchase offer. No manual work, no separate fulfillment flow.

        Charge For It, Or Don’t

        Offer gift notes free, or turn them into a paid add-on at checkout. Either way, setup takes about three minutes from the Unbox app — no Shopify field mapping, no engineering ticket. Choose how much to charge, what buyers see and whether it appears in cart, checkout, or both. It’s a setting, not a project.
